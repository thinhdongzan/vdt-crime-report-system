# Design System: Hệ thống Tiếp nhận và Điều phối Thông tin tố giác tội phạm
**Project ID:** 12852374310112598801

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The atmosphere is highly formal, serious, and authoritative, suitable for a government or public sector application. The layout emphasizes clarity, readability, and structured hierarchy to instill trust and security. Visuals are clean and unadorned, avoiding playful, startup-like, or overly modern trends. A subtle, low-opacity (4%-10%) traditional Vietnamese Dong Son drum pattern serves as a watermark in key background areas to provide cultural identity without distracting from the content.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Deep Vietnamese Red** (#8B0000): Used for primary branding (header backgrounds, strong accents) and primary action buttons. Conveys authority and official capacity.
* **Dark Red** (#5C0000): Used for hover states on primary actions and deep background elements for contrast.
* **Soft Red Background** (#F8EAEA): Used for subtle highlights, alert backgrounds, or active states to maintain color harmony without overwhelming.
* **Muted Bronze/Gold Accent** (#C9A227): Used sparingly for decorative accents, active indicators, or premium trust badges.
* **Ivory Background** (#F7F5F0): The main page background color, providing a warm, approachable, but formal base that is easier on the eyes than stark white.
* **Surface White** (#FFFFFF): Used for foreground elements like content cards, forms, and input fields to ensure maximum legibility and contrast against the ivory background.
* **Subtle Border** (#E5E0D8): Used to define structural elements like cards, dividers, and input borders without introducing harsh lines.
* **Main Text Charcoal** (#1F2933): Used for all primary typography (headings and body text) to ensure strong readability and high contrast.
* **Muted Text Slate** (#6B7280): Used for secondary information, subtitles, placeholders, and descriptions.

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Font Family:** Inter (or similar clean sans-serif like Roboto).
* **Usage:** Clean, professional typography with strong legibility. 
* **Headings:** Solid and structural, devoid of decorative styling. Weights lean towards medium or semi-bold.
* **Body Text:** Clear, airy, and easy to read for long forms and descriptive texts.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** 
  - Shape: Subtly rounded corners (4px radius) for a structured and rigid official feel.
  - Primary: Deep Vietnamese Red (#8B0000) background with white text.
  - Secondary/Tertiary: Transparent or Surface White (#FFFFFF) with Deep Vietnamese Red or Muted Text Slate text/borders.
* **Cards/Containers:** 
  - Shape: Subtly rounded corners (4px radius).
  - Background: Surface White (#FFFFFF) resting on the Ivory Background (#F7F5F0).
  - Depth: Flat with subtle, whisper-soft diffused shadows or clean borders (#E5E0D8) for separation.
* **Inputs/Forms:** 
  - Shape: Subtly rounded corners (4px radius).
  - Style: Surface White (#FFFFFF) background with a Subtle Border (#E5E0D8) stroke. Large, readable text padding.

## 5. Layout Principles
* **Structure:** Highly organized with a clear grid system. Space is used generously to prevent clutter and ensure inputs/actions are unmistakable.
* **Hierarchy:** Distinct visual separation between navigation, hero messaging, and actionable areas.
* **Responsiveness:** Stacks vertically on mobile to maintain clarity, with the login page placing branding on top and the form below.
